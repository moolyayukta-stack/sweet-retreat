Our Story

Baked at home, delivered with care

Sweet Retreat started as one home baker's love for getting a cake exactly right — the flavour, the finish, the little details that make it feel personal. Every order is still baked in small batches, by hand, with the same care as the first one.

Whether it's a birthday, a baby shower, a wedding or just a Tuesday that needs cake — we work with you on the flavour, the filling and the design until it matches what you had in mind.
